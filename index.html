The Problem

Your Growth Stack Has Amnesia

Every tool solves one task. No tool remembers the campaign. The problem isn't generating AI output. It's that nothing holds it together.

  • Your SEO agent and content agent have never spoken to each other. Insights learned by one never reach the others — contradictory strategies run in parallel while you pay for both.
  • The freelancer you hired last month took their learnings with them. Institutional knowledge walks out the door every time, and the next campaign starts from zero.
  • Campaign #4 starts from scratch, just like Campaign #1. No compounding, no learning curve — growth stays linear when it could be exponential.
  • You have 7 tools but no single source of truth. Notion, Clay, Buffer, Ahrefs — nothing talks to anything. You become the glue between all of them.
