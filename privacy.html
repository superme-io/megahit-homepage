This policy is a structural starting draft, not final legal copy. It must be reviewed and finalized by qualified legal counsel before publishing, reflecting actual data practices, the vendor/subprocessor list, and applicable privacy laws (e.g., GDPR, CCPA).

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Super Intelligence, operator of Megahit GrowthOS ("Megahit," "we," "us"), collects, uses, and shares information when you use megahit.ai and related services. Effective date: July 12, 2026.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account and contact information: name, email, company.
  • Usage data: how the product is used.
  • Campaign and growth data you input or connect (e.g., CRM, analytics integrations).
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies.

3. How We Use Information

To provide and improve the service, to power the AI agents and memory layer, for billing, for support, and for marketing communications (with opt-out).

4. How We Share Information

With subprocessors and vendors (e.g., cloud hosting, LLM providers used to power agents). We do not sell personal data. We disclose information only as required by law or with consent.

5. Cookies and Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-FRR3PME1TR) to understand how visitors find and use megahit.ai. Google sets first-party cookies (for example _ga and _ga_<stream-id>) containing a randomly generated identifier used to distinguish returning visitors. Through this we collect pages viewed, session duration, referring source, approximate geographic location derived from IP address, and device and browser type. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store full IP addresses. We also use Google Search Console, which reports aggregated search queries that lead to this site and sets no cookies.

You can opt out by blocking cookies in your browser or by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. We do not currently operate a cookie consent banner; consent mechanics for visitors in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom are pending the legal review noted above.

6. Data Retention

Account data, campaign data, and memory/context data are retained while your account is active. On cancellation, outputs and performance data remain accessible for 90 days and are exportable.

7. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, or portability of your personal data by emailing business@megahit.ai. Regional rights (GDPR/CCPA) apply as confirmed by counsel.

8. Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your data. (To be detailed with actual practices.)

9. International Data Transfers

Where data crosses borders, we rely on appropriate transfer safeguards. (To be confirmed by counsel.)

10. Children's Privacy

The service is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect children's data.
