Real questions. Direct answers.

What's the difference between Agent Mode and Agency Mode?

Agent Mode: AI leads, you supervise — humans step in on edge cases only, you're still in the driver's seat. Agency Mode: you set a goal, we deliver it — operators handle everything with AI doing the scale work, you check results not tasks.

Why does Agency Mode cost 10× Agent Mode?

Because we own the outcome, not just the output. Traditional agencies charge $20K–$100K/month and still make you manage the work. We take it off your plate entirely — and we get smarter every month instead of resetting.

Is Market Research really free? What's the catch?

Yes, permanently free — no card, no trial period. 20 minutes of setup, first report within hours.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Outputs and performance data remain accessible for 90 days and are exportable. The Megahit memory layer — ICP learnings, campaign history, creator relationships — is purpose-built for Megahit and doesn't port elsewhere; that's why month 6 is dramatically more valuable than month 1.

How fast will I see results?

Outbound: reply rates/meetings visible within 2 weeks. KOL: attributed traffic/signups in 3–6 weeks. Market Research: same day. Performance improves significantly by month 3 as the system calibrates.

Can I start with Agent Mode and upgrade later?

Yes — recommended path. Everything transfers seamlessly to Agency Mode when ready; no re-briefing.
