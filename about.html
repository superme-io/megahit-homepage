Chapter I

The Problem We Lived

We were the founders this product is for. We ran the SEO tool, the outbound tool, the content tool, the analytics tool — and watched them each do their one job competently while the campaign as a whole went nowhere. We hired agencies that produced beautiful quarterly decks, then restarted from a blank brief every quarter as if the last one hadn't happened. Freelancers came, learned, left — and took everything they learned with them.

Somewhere along the way we realized we had become the infrastructure. The founder was the API between tools, the memory between campaigns, the judgment between drafts. That's not a growth strategy. That's a coping mechanism.

"The problem isn't execution. Plenty of things get done. The problem is that nothing connects. Nothing learns. And you're the one holding it all together."

Why AI × Human

AI gives you scale — a thousand drafts, around-the-clock monitoring, instant analysis. Humans give you judgment — the reply that saves a deal, the negotiation instinct, the taste. A memory layer that remembers everything makes both compound. That combination is the company. As we said in the earliest pitch: "Fast like AI. Accountable like a great hire."

Chapter IV

How Megahit Actually Works

We start with a real intake, not an onboarding form.

When a founder starts with Megahit, a Planning Agent runs a deep intake: stage, ICP, competitive position, past experiments, near-term goals. It builds a full campaign plan from this foundation — milestones, agent assignments, cost estimates, timeline — spanning up to six months. This isn't a list of tactics; it's a dependency graph the system can act on without waiting for you to approve every step.

The agent acts before you ask — and knows when not to.

Once the plan is approved, an Orchestrator continuously monitors performance signals and dispatches the next task the moment a trigger condition is met — no waiting for a weekly check-in. Every task is classified by stakes and confidence at planning time: low-stakes, high-confidence work runs fully automated; anything that needs judgment (a KOL negotiation, a reply that could sour a deal) routes to a human operator through a Human Routing Engine, with full context already attached.

What your team confirms becomes what every future agent knows.

Every approval, edit, or rejection you make is captured as a labeled signal and synced back into the system in real time — not just once at onboarding. When results diverge from the original plan, a Re-planning Engine updates the remaining roadmap automatically, using what was just learned rather than discarding it. Campaign #2 starts from a position of strength, not zero.

"Tell us your goal. We build the program, run the campaigns, handle the relationships, and show up next month knowing more than we did last month."
Chapter V

Why This Is Different From Everything Else

We act before you ask.

Most growth tools and agencies wait for a brief, a login, or a monthly check-in. Megahit's agents monitor performance continuously and act on triggers the moment they appear — drafting the next test, flagging the next opportunity — then sync what happened with your team instead of waiting to be asked.

We track what actually converts, not what looks good.

Every campaign output is tracked against real downstream outcomes, not opens or impressions. A 60% open rate converting at 0.3% is worse than a 40% rate converting at 2.1% — and Megahit is built to know the difference and act on it, not just report it.

Real human operators with real relationships.

Our operators have genuine relationships in creator networks, media outlets, and specialist communities — not a cold outreach template. When a KOL deal or press placement matters, we're negotiating from an existing relationship — faster deals, cleaner terms, and creators who genuinely endorse the product instead of posting a disclosure.

We own the outcome, not just the output.

Most tools and agencies deliver a deliverable and stop. Megahit monitors what happens after a campaign goes live, synthesizes results across channels, and comes back next month with a plan adjusted for what was learned — the job doesn't end when the task is marked complete.
